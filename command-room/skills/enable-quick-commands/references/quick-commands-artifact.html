Daily Loop · Morning Pattern
What to ask first thing. Each fires a chat that surfaces what matters.
what's going on
Full daily briefing across calendar, inbox, commitments. Use first thing in the morning.
let's work
Silent project-list load — no briefing, no questions. Use when you already know what you need.
brief me
Daily digest: calendar, important email summary, overdue follow-ups, urgent items.
triage my inbox
Classifies overnight email into Reply Now / Decision / FYI / Discard. Drafts 2-3 replies.
Workspace · Navigate
Load a project, list what's active, search across history.
go [name]
Fuzzy-match into any project, person, or org. Aliases work. Replace [name].
list active
Zero-interaction org tree of every active project with last-activity dates.
show muted
Every live snooze and mute, with time remaining and a one-tap Unmute.
search transcripts for [topic]
Cross-meeting keyword search. Returns hits with date, attendees, and context snippet.
People · Relationships
Never walk into a meeting cold. Owns the relationship layer.
tell me about [person]
Full person record — who they are, recent activity, open commitments, history.
prep me for dinner with [person]
Casual-context brief — what's on their mind, what to ask, what not to bring up.
prep for my 1:1 with [person]
Direct-report brief: what they own, open commitments, pattern flags (overloaded / drifting).
intro [A] to [B]
Two voice-calibrated intro drafts (double-opt-in + direct). Logged into the connections graph.
who went dark
Customers quiet relative to their own cadence. Ranked list with re-engagement angles.
threads to revive
Warm conversations that died mid-step. Different from dormant-customer-scan (people vs threads).
balance check
Personal white space — the person outside work who's gone quiet, pinned to a real open evening. Private to you; needs a connected personal calendar.
Deals · Pipeline
Every open deal — stage, next step, what's rotting, what's closing.
show my pipeline
Ranked deal report — value, stage clocks, missing next steps, one-tap moves per deal.
new deal [name] with [org]
Open a deal on an existing org — stage, value, expected close. The org itself stays untouched.
pipeline review
The weekly pass — what moved, what stalled, what closed, and the top three moves to make.
Drafts · Writing
Voice-calibrated output. Each saves a .docx or stages a Gmail draft.
draft an email to [person] about [topic]
Voice-matched draft staged to Gmail Drafts. Replace brackets.
memo on [topic]
1-3 page structured memo — decision doc, scope, strategy, board update.
write a one-pager on [topic]
Polished .docx — headline, 3 key points, supporting data, recommendation. Under 60s.
decision memo on [topic]
Structured tradeoff analysis — options, weighted criteria, recommendation. Three-pass interactive.
build the board pack
Multi-page board pack .docx — KPIs, deltas, wins, concerns, decisions, asks, hiring, financials.
Meetings · Process & Follow-up
Turn meetings into events, decisions, and follow-ups.
prep me for my next meeting
Synthesizes calendar + inbox + Slack + transcripts + commitments for every attendee. Use 5 min before.
process the last call
Pulls most recent Granola transcript — extracts events, commitments, decisions.
follow up on the [meeting] meeting
60-second close-the-loop pack: summary, per-attendee action items, voice-calibrated drafts.
schedule a 30-min with [name]
Finds mutual availability, drafts substrate-aware agenda, creates the calendar event.
Memory · Decisions & Insights
Compounds over time. Read these to track your own thinking.
log decision: [what]
Append to the decision log with rationale. Use for any choice you'll want to remember why.
what decisions should I revisit
Surfaces stale decisions based on elapsed time + contradictory signal. Per-decision actions.
clean up my workspace
Self-maintaining weekly tidy-up — heals drift, fixes what's safe, flags only what needs your eyes.
generate insights
Weekly synthesis — cross-project patterns, what's drifting, classification review, project proposals.
weekly recap
7 days across every connector synthesized into a .docx + inline summary. Passive backfill side-effect.
scan for commitments
One-shot backfill from historic transcripts + Gmail. Use when commitments tracker shows empty.
Strategy · Analysis
Pressure-test plans, contracts, and the workflows hiding in your week.
stress test this plan
Munger inversion — every failure mode mapped, then reversed into structural safeguards.
review this contract
Extracts terms, compares to your standard, flags deviations green/yellow/red, suggests redlines.
what can be automated
Scans session notes + transcripts for repetitive work. Ranks by time-saved-vs-build-effort.
scaffold automation #1
Generates real working artifacts (Zap config, script, n8n flow) for a surfaced opportunity.
Ingest · Pull In Data
Bring outside context into the workspace.
ingest folder [path]
Auto-detects intent — extracts context, optionally copies files into matching projects.
ingest context from [path]
Extract people / projects / decisions only — no file copies. Use for ChatGPT exports, v1.x migrations.
file documents from [path]
Context extraction PLUS file migration into project folders. Copy-only, preview-before-confirm.
break down this video [url]
Turns any link or pasted content into structured intel connected to named entities.
Reporting
CEO-facing recaps of what the system did for you and what it cost.
monthly recap
Operating Lift report — what would have slipped, what got captured, conservative hours-saved estimate.
value receipt
Forwardable monthly ROI receipt — counts plus a conservative hours figure, no names. Add "quarterly" for the roll-up.
usage report
Plain-English breakdown of scheduled-task spend — per orchestrator, per connector, per fire.
staff meeting
The weekly review in one sitting — what the system changed on its own, everything waiting on your yes/no, and this week's relationship moves. One tap each.
what did you change
Read-only feed of what the system did without being asked — closures from your sent mail, recovered items, expired suggestions — each with an undo.
