# The coach's knowledge library

SPEC SURFACES2_11c Lane 3 item 4. These are the ONLY lines the End of Day
coach may put on a screen as a bigger-picture reading. The evening picks at
most one of them per close and at most two in an ISO week, on a coached seat
only, and it picks by the pattern the evening actually found — never at
random and never twice in a row from the same shelf.

## The rules every line here obeys

1. **A source, always** — author and work, in words. No URL: a link is a
   thing to go and read, and this is one sentence at the end of somebody's
   day. The author is cited by SURNAME, the way a citation does it, and for
   a second reason: plugin source carries no first-name/surname pairs at all
   (the privacy guard enforces that fleet-wide), and a rule with one
   exception for famous people is a rule with one exception.
2. **No quotation longer than fifteen words**, and by preference none at all.
   Every line below is a paraphrase of the idea in the coach's own voice, so
   nothing here reproduces anybody's prose.
3. **No figure** — no counts, no percentages, no "N of M". A reading with a
   number in it reads as a measurement of the seat, and `claims.scan_surface`
   refuses it on this surface.
4. **No instruction and no judgement.** A line says how something tends to
   work, not what the reader should do about it tonight.
5. **One line per entry**, written to stand alone under a pattern sentence.

## The file's shape

Each entry is one bullet under a pattern heading, with the line and its
source separated by ` :: `. `eod_coach.read_knowledge` is the only reader.

## stillness

- Making time visible is the first move, because time spends itself whether or not anybody is watching. :: Drucker, The Effective Executive
- A system's output comes from how the work flows, not from how hard anybody tries. :: Deming, Out of the Crisis

## mention

- The gap between what a room discusses and what a room does stays open until somebody names it. :: Argyris, Overcoming Organizational Defenses
- A meeting is the medium of managerial work, and it is judged by what leaves it. :: Grove, High Output Management

## survival

- Plans run long because we picture the version where nothing gets in the way. :: Kahneman, Thinking, Fast and Slow
- A queue lengthens at its slowest step, not at its busiest one. :: Goldratt, The Goal

## resources

If you want someone to talk to right now, a local crisis or support line is the fastest
route — in the US you can call or text 988; elsewhere, your local emergency number reaches
someone who can help.
