# Decision Memo — structural exemplar (kind: decision_memo)

> STRUCTURE CONTRACT — annotations are the exemplar; sample lines are
> synthetic. Layout only (contract > exemplar > default).
>
> - Header: VERDICT-only lead (eyebrow-excluded). Verdict = picked option +
>   its condition + the decide-by date, one sentence.
> - Ordering rule (enforced): Recommendation sits directly after Framing,
>   ABOVE the comparison — a rec-headed section past index 2 refuses to
>   render. The interactive weight-setting flow is unchanged; only the
>   document order is decision-forward.
> - Visuals: Criteria & weights is a table (Criterion | Weight | Why);
>   the Comparison is a matrix with the recommended option's column
>   highlighted — no blank cells, "n/a" where a cell has no data.
> - Ask close: the NEEDED reader-action rides the verdict sentence or the
>   widget; no separate prose ask section.
