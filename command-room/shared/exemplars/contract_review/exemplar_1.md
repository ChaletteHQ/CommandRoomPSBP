# Contract Review — structural exemplar (kind: contract_review)

> STRUCTURE CONTRACT — annotations are the exemplar; sample lines are
> synthetic. Layout only (contract > exemplar > default).
>
> - Header: VERDICT-only lead (eyebrow-excluded). Verdict = the deal-breaker
>   flag line — or "Safe to sign as drafted" when nothing is red.
> - Visuals: "How It Compares To Your Standard" is a flag MATRIX
>   (Your standard | This contract | Flag), each Flag cell tinted by
>   severity. Flags are WORDS — Standard / Review / Flag — never emoji or
>   color names; the tint must survive grayscale printing.
> - The flag matrix is the scan-in-5-seconds layer; prose analysis lives
>   below it in Suggested Language, never inside the matrix.
> - Ask close: the NEEDED reader-action ("approve the [clause] redline")
>   rides the verdict or the widget; no separate ask section.
