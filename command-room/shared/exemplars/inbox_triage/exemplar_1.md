# Inbox Triage — structural exemplar (kind: inbox_triage)

> STRUCTURE CONTRACT — annotations are the exemplar; sample lines are
> synthetic. Layout only (contract > exemplar > default).
>
> - Header: FULL 3-line eyebrow. This is brief-family — a digest of what
>   arrived since the last pass, not a one-shot analysis. Verdict = what the
>   inbox amounts to in one line, the same sentence the chat return opens with.
> - Length tracks the INBOX, not effort. A quiet morning is a short brief and
>   that is correct; never pad the ranked list to reach five.
> - Visuals: header stat-tile band (unread · flagged to read first · replies
>   drafted — drop-empty, a real zero renders); the bucket accounting is a
>   compact list, one line per bucket, counts included.
> - Ranked-list grammar (every item in "Top of the Pile"): bucket · sender
>   (org) · subject in the sender's own words · state or next act. Cap five —
>   everything else lives in the bucket lists below.
> - Reply Drafts names recipient + subject ONLY. The draft bodies live in the
>   chat widget, which is the editing surface; the document does not duplicate
>   them.
> - Ask close: the drafts widget IS the ask surface. No prose "What I need
>   from you" twin.
> - Scannable in 60 seconds.
