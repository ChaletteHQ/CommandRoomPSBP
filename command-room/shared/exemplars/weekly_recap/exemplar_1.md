# Weekly Recap — structural exemplar (kind: weekly_recap)

> STRUCTURE CONTRACT — annotations are the exemplar; sample lines are
> synthetic. Layout only (contract > exemplar > default).
>
> - Header: FULL 3-line eyebrow (verdict + CHANGED / DECIDE / NEEDED); a
>   sanctioned synthesis-lead surface — verdict = the week's one conclusion.
>   CHANGED carries the week-over-week deltas. Subtitle (the date window) is
>   required.
> - Target length: scales with signal — ~20 lines on a quiet week, 50–60 on a
>   heavy one. Empty sections are OMITTED, never padded.
> - Visuals: "The week in numbers" opening stat-tile band (1–5 tiles,
>   drop-empty); the two owe sections render as two-column tables capped at
>   10 rows + "+N more". Everything else is prose or short bullets.
> - Grouping: close with "By project" OR "By day" — one, never both.
> - Ask close: 0–3 reader-actions.
