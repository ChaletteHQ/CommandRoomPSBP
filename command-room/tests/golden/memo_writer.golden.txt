MEMO
Acme Co — Sourcing Bot — Pricing
Strategy memo · <DATE> · Acme Co
Price the Sourcing Bot at value, not cost.
Decision
Move Sourcing Bot to annual-only billing and lock the pricing tiers before the Northstar renewal.
Context
Pricing has been the recurring open question across three logged decisions. The Northstar renewal forces the question now.
Options considered
Option | Pros | Cons
Per-seat monthly | Simple to explain | Revenue is lumpy
Annual-only | Predictable revenue | Higher friction to close
Usage-based | Scales with value | Hard to forecast
Rationale
Annual-only smooths revenue ahead of the renewal.
Tiered seats keep the entry price honest.
Locking tiers now removes a renewal-time negotiation variable.

Send the pricing sheet to the Northstar team.
